## Design Problem Identified

**Issue**: Too many glyphs with harsh straight lines
- ⟋⎯⎯ (Zero) - Too mechanical
- └⎯⎯ (Learning Rate) - Too rigid  
- ⎯⊥⎯ (Tripartite) - Too angular
- ⊔⎯ (Unification) - Too boxy
- ╋ (Entanglement) - Plain cross (needs ankh influence)
- ⊢─ (Fitness) - Too stark

**Solution**: Redesign with flowing, organic curves inspired by Egyptian glyphs
- Ankh elements (life, binding)
- Eye of Horus curves (vision, pattern)
- Scarab beetle (transformation, evolution)
- Lotus curves (growth, emergence)
- Was scepter (power, control)
- Papyrus stems (flexibility, data flow)

## Font Rendering Notes

### Curve Smoothness

```yaml
All revised glyphs use Bezier curves:
  
Quadratic Bezier (simple curves):
  - ⊶ (Fitness hook)
  - ⌙ (Learning curve)
  - ⊍ (Unification base)

Cubic Bezier (complex curves):
  - ☥ (Ankh loop - perfect circle)
  - ⊹ (Tripartite branches)
  - ⟓ (Zero circle - hollow)

Rendering Quality:
  - Minimum resolution: 16pt
  - Optimal: 24pt and above
  - Curves smooth at all sizes
  - Print quality: 300 DPI+
```

### Stroke Consistency

```yaml
All glyphs maintain consistent stroke width:
  
Primary strokes: 80 units
Secondary: 60 units
Accents: 40 units

Curved sections:
  - Same optical weight as straight
  - Compensate for curve thinning
  - Consistent visual density

Testing:
  - Render at multiple sizes
  - Check stroke uniformity
  - Verify curve smoothness
```

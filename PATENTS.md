## Overview

This file identifies patent claims and patent applications associated with the
APLO platform. It is provided in the spirit of transparency under the APLO ISA
open specification (see `LICENSE_ISA.md`) and to define the boundaries of
the patent grant included in that license.

## Patent Defensive Commitment

Aevov Corporation commits that it will not assert any of the patents listed in
the "Included in ISA Open License Grant" section against:

- Any academic or research implementation of the APLO ISA
- Any open-source toolchain implementing the canonical APLO ISA Specification
- NASA, ESA, JAXA, ISRO, or other government space agencies implementing the
  APLO ISA for non-commercial missions

This commitment is irrevocable with respect to implementations that comply with
the canonical APLO ISA Specification as published at the time of implementation.

## Third-Party Patent Notices

APLO's dimensional algebra type system draws on mathematical structures (E₆, E₈
lattices, octonions, sedenions) that are in the public domain as mathematical
abstractions. No third-party patent claims are known to read on the APLO ISA
Specification as of the date of this filing.

Quantum computing gate implementations (Hadamard, CNOT, Grover oracle) are based
on well-established quantum computing theory. APLO's novel contribution is the
encoding and representation of these operations, not the underlying mathematics.
